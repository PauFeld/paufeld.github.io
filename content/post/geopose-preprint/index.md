New preprint out: [GeoPose: Patient-agnostic CTA-to-DSA registration through projection-space calibration](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.16600), with Rudolf L. M. van Herten, Robert Graf, and Johannes C. Paetzold. GeoPose is a population-trained framework for registering intraoperative angiography (DSA) to pre-procedural CT angiography (CTA), achieving fast, accurate registration without patient-specific adaptation. See the [publication page](/publication/feldman-2026-geopose/) for details.
