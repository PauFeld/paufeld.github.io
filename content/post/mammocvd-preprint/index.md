New preprint out: [Mammography Foundation Models for Opportunistic Prediction of Major Adverse Cardiovascular Events](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.19385), with Nusrat Binta Nizam, Sunwoo Kwak, Batuhan Karaman, Katerina Dodelzon, and Mert R. Sabuncu. We show that mammography foundation models pretrained for breast cancer tasks can predict 5-year cardiovascular risk directly from a screening mammogram, without cardiovascular labels or breast arterial calcification annotations. Code is available at [github.com/PauFeld/MammoCVD](https://github.com/PauFeld/MammoCVD). See the [publication page](/publication/feldman-2026-mammocvd/) for details.
