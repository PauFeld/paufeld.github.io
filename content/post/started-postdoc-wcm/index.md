I joined Weill Cornell Medicine as a Postdoctoral Associate in the [Sabuncu Lab](https://sabuncu.engineering.cornell.edu/), under the supervision of Mert Sabuncu. My work now focuses on machine learning methods for extracting clinically meaningful information from medical images, with particular interests in cardiovascular and breast imaging, foundation models, and self-supervised learning.
