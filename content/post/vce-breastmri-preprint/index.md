New preprint out: [Toward Contrast-Free Breast MRI: Improving Cancer Detection With Virtual Contrast Enhanced Imaging](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.00140), led by Hongxi Chen with Sunwoo Kwak, Sanyukta Adap, Katerina Dodelzon, and Mert R. Sabuncu. Combining non-contrast MRI with AI-generated virtual contrast-enhanced images raises cancer detection AUROC from 0.865 to 0.901, and a simulated routing strategy could limit acquired contrast imaging to around 40% of patients while preserving diagnostic performance. See the [publication page](/publication/feldman-2026-vce-breastmri/) for details.
